Privacy Policy for TableSweep

Last updated: November 20, 2026

TableSweep runs locally inside your browser with minimal permissions and no tracking or analytics.

1. Data Collection and Webpage Content

TableSweep does not collect, transmit, sell, or store any webpage contents, extracted tables, or browsing history on external servers. All table detection, formatting, and file exports (CSV, TSV, XLSX, JSON, Markdown) execute locally on your device.

2. Permissions and Storage Used

  • activeTab and scripting: Used only to detect and highlight tables, grids, or lists on the currently active tab when you click the TableSweep toolbar icon or start the visual picker.
  • storage (local and session): Used to store your local export preferences (such as CSV delimiter and BOM settings), daily free-export counts, and temporary per-tab multi-page batch rows during your browser session.
  • storage (Chrome Sync): If you activate a TableSweep Pro license key while signed into Chrome Sync, the license record is stored in chrome.storage.sync so Google Chrome can sync your entitlement across your signed-in browsers.

3. Payments and Licensing (Pro Version)

Payments and license keys are processed by Polar.sh (Merchant of Record). TableSweep does not collect or store payment card numbers. When you enter a license key to unlock Pro features, only the license key and organization identifier are sent directly to Polar's validation endpoint (https://api.polar.sh) to confirm entitlement.

4. Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact the developer using the support contact listed on the TableSweep Chrome Web Store page.
